Video validation issue detection criteria

Native video player checks:
1. Video source presence
   - Fails if the visible video element has no src, currentSrc, or <source> candidate.

2. Video hidden by CSS
   - Fails if the player is user-facing but not visually rendered (zero size, display:none, invisible, etc.).

3. Video clipped or partially hidden
   - Fails if the visible video area is clipped or partially outside the viewport.

4. Broken Video URL
   - Fails when the video source URL returns a non-success HTTP status (404/500/etc.).

5. Incorrect Video MIME Type
   - Fails when the source responds with a non-video MIME type.

6. Browser codec unsupported
   - Fails when the browser reports the supplied video format is not playable.

7. CORS Playback Failure
   - Fails when browser security blocks media loading due to cross-origin/CORS issues.

8. Play Button Disabled
   - Fails when a visible play control exists but is disabled.

9. Controls Not Available
   - Fails when a visible video has no native controls, no visible custom play button, and does not autoplay.

10. Autoplay Blocked / Play Button Not Responding / Video Never Starts
    - Fails when playback is attempted but currentTime does not advance.

11. Video Stalled During Playback
    - Fails when playback starts but then stops advancing during observation.

12. Black Video Frame
    - Fails when the video area remains mostly black after playback begins.

13. Audio Plays But Video Is Frozen
    - Fails when audio progresses but visible frames do not change.

14. Controls Disappear
    - Fails when native controls vanish after playback starts.

15. Infinite Loading Spinner
    - Fails when a loading indicator stays visible and the video does not become playable.

16. Poster Image Stuck
    - Fails when a poster/thumbnail overlay remains visible after playback is requested.

Embedded video checks:
1. Embedded video source presence
   - Fails if the iframe has no valid YouTube/Vimeo source URL.

2. Video hidden by CSS
   - Fails if the embedded iframe is hidden or has no visible size.

3. Video clipped or partially hidden
   - Fails if the embedded player is cut off or outside the viewport.

4. Embedded Video Failed To Load
   - Fails if the iframe source URL returns HTTP 4xx/5xx.

5. Embedded Video Failed To Load (security/blocking)
   - Fails if browser security or provider restrictions block the embedded player.

Severity guidance:
- Most video failures are classified as High.
- Layout/visibility clipping issues are typically Medium.
- Source absence and playback failures are treated as High or Critical depending on context.
